# Degree Atlas — Unified Academic Content Specification

## Purpose
Build a syllabus-first, research-grade learning companion for IGNOU MA Anthropology (July 2026), undergraduate-to-MUJ MSc Mathematics (Computational Science pathway), and interdisciplinary Complexity Science. The public application must show academic content, not implementation prompts.

## Course guides
Every course should provide the verified official code, credits and syllabus, one best primary textbook, suitable alternatives, a direct open learning course where one exists, prerequisites, a coherent chapter progression, exercises, assignments, notes, and links to related research. Where no exact open course or syllabus-matched textbook has been verified, label the resource as a supplementary starting point rather than an official match.

## Books
Books are not limited to 25 per field. Organise by course and by beginner → intermediate → advanced → research, with prerequisites, recommended reading order, specific chapters, why to read, how to practise and next-step criteria. Fiction is supplemental where genuinely relevant. Distinguish open access from catalogue/publisher access.

## Six distinct bibliographies
- Anthropology: 100 verified research papers; 100 verified essays/articles.
- Mathematics: 100 verified research papers; 100 verified essays/articles.
- Complexity Science: 100 verified research papers; 100 verified essays/articles.

**Target: 600 individually verified records.** No padding with books, invented titles or generic searches. Each record needs title, author, publication date, venue, DOI or direct URL, publication type, subject and subdiscipline, difficulty, linked course/topic, why it matters, reading sequence, and original summary. Papers and essays/articles must never be mixed. Provide chronological and concept-based browsing.

## Learning
Offer evidence-grounded retrieval practice, spaced practice, self-explanation, worked examples, error journaling, active recall, deliberate practice, learning methods, memory palaces and review plans. Connect to actual study exercises.

## Research
Build interdisciplinary concept maps, modelling exercises, reproducible simulation projects, research questions and literature pathways. Distinguish research hypotheses from verified contributions.

## Integrity and implementation
University deadlines must come from applicable official notices or the student's LMS, not guesses. Keep sources, date checked and uncertainty. Preserve user progress. Use GitHub Pages and local backups; no simulated cloud sync or fake AI integrations.

## Current implementation status
Course-specific reference and open-resource maps have been added for 23 IGNOU courses, 21 MUJ courses and 12 undergraduate foundation modules. A separately classified seed bibliography of 18 source-linked readings has been added. The complete 600-item verification target, chapter-by-chapter syllabus alignment, and full reading pathways remain outstanding; do not claim they are finished.
